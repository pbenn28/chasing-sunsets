The bill directs NIST to develop a program for the voluntary reporting of AI flaws and vulnerabilities and to accelerate their detection and monitoring, creating a coordinated channel for tracking AI system weaknesses to improve security. It was among ten AI bills advanced by the House Science Committee in June 2026.
