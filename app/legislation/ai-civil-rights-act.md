The bill establishes protections against algorithmic discrimination by “covered algorithms” that make or contribute to consequential decisions — housing, employment, healthcare, credit, and more. It requires pre-deployment evaluations and post-deployment impact assessments, notice and disclosure to affected individuals, and a private right of action, with enforcement by the FTC and state attorneys general. A companion Senate version from Sen. Markey exists in the same Congress.
