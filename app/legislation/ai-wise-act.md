The bill requires the Small Business Administration, within 180 days of enactment and in consultation with NIST and an advisory working group of AI experts, small-business outreach specialists, and educational content developers, to establish and maintain publicly available educational resources and online learning modules explaining how AI tools work, their limitations and risks, how to identify AI-generated outputs, how to manage AI-related risks and privacy, and how to evaluate whether adopting a given AI tool is worthwhile. Materials must be model-neutral — not favoring any particular AI provider — and disseminated through existing SBA resource partners such as Small Business Development Centers, Women's Business Centers, and SCORE. The bill authorizes no additional appropriations. It passed the full House by voice vote under suspension of the rules on 2026-01-20 and is now pending before the Senate Small Business and Entrepreneurship Committee.
