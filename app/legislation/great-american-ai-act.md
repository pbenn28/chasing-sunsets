GAAIA is the most ambitious AI proposal in Congress by page count — a 269-page bipartisan discussion draft spanning four titles: Frontier AI Governance, Workforce, Cybersecurity, and Research/International Cooperation. It would require large frontier AI developers (those with more than $500 million in annual revenue) to disclose safety information and undergo independent audits, formally establish a Center for AI Standards and Innovation inside NIST/Commerce (roughly $100 million a year), and preempt certain state laws that regulate how AI models are *developed* for three years, while leaving state authority over how AI is *used* largely intact.

It has never been formally introduced as a bill — it remains a draft out for stakeholder feedback — and by late July 2026 its own authors had already peeled off its most defensible piece (frontier-model oversight) into the standalone FRONTIER Act, a common signal that the larger package is struggling to find committee support as a whole.
