The LIFT AI Act authorizes the NSF to support programs improving AI literacy for K-12 students and educators, including learning materials and teacher training. It was among the AI bills advanced by the House Science Committee in June 2026. As of early September 2026, it remains at committee-passed status with no House Rules Committee action or floor vote scheduled.
