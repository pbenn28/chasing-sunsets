The bill would direct the Secretary of Energy to establish an “Advanced Artificial Intelligence Evaluation Program” within the Department of Energy to test advanced and high-impact AI systems for risks such as weaponization and loss of control before deployment. Developers would have to submit qualifying models for evaluation, and the program would report annually to Congress with recommendations on managing AI-related risks, including artificial superintelligence.
