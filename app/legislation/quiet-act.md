The QUIET Act would amend the Communications Act of 1934 to require robocalls using AI to emulate a human to include a disclosure at the start of the message that AI is being used. It also doubles the maximum forfeiture penalty and criminal fine for certain TCPA violations involving AI voice or text-message impersonation.
