The bill would direct the GAO to conduct a technology assessment of liquid-cooling systems for AI compute clusters and high-performance computing facilities and develop government-wide best-practice guidance for federal agencies. The Secretary of Energy would then evaluate the assessment and advise Congress on liquid cooling's role in maintaining U.S. AI competitiveness.
