The bill would make it U.S. policy to promote export of the full American AI technology “stack” — semiconductors, cloud infrastructure, AI models, data pipelines, and technical standards — bundled to trusted foreign partners. It directs the State-chaired Economic Diplomacy Action Group to open foreign markets and provides selected industry consortia with federal financing, expedited export licensing, and diplomatic support. It is the accelerationist counterpoint to the export-restriction bills tracked here.
