The bill directs the Secretary of State to submit annual assessments to Congress for four years on risks posed by foreign adversaries' malicious use of generative AI, including synthetic media and foreign malign-influence operations. Assessments would be unclassified with an optional classified annex, and unclassified portions posted publicly.
