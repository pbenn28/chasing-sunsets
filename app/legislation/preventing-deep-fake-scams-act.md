The bill would establish a Treasury-chaired Task Force on Artificial Intelligence in the Financial Services Sector to study AI-enabled fraud — including deepfakes and voice/image cloning used to compromise consumer financial accounts — and report to Congress with standardized AI definitions, risk descriptions, and best practices to protect institutions and consumers.
