The bill would direct NIST and the State Department to encourage and enable U.S. participation in international standards-setting for AI and other critical and emerging technologies. It requires a NIST report to Congress on current U.S. participation, creates a web portal to help stakeholders track and engage in standardization efforts, and establishes a four-year, $10 million pilot grant program to support hosting standards meetings in the United States — with a further report to Congress on the program's effectiveness. A predecessor version (S. 3849) was introduced in the 118th Congress without advancing.
