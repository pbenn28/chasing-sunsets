The bill would prohibit employment retaliation against current and former AI-industry employees who disclose AI security vulnerabilities or violations of law related to AI, providing remedies including reinstatement, back pay, and compensatory damages. It is described as the first federal legislation specifically protecting AI-safety whistleblowers. As of September 2026, the House companion (H.R. 3460, Obernolte/Lieu) sits in the House Committee on Education and Workforce, not Energy and Commerce as previously listed; neither chamber has taken committee action beyond the initial referral.
