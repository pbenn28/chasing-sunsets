The bill would prohibit employment retaliation against current and former AI-industry employees who disclose AI security vulnerabilities or violations of law related to AI, providing remedies including reinstatement, back pay, and compensatory damages. It is described as the first federal legislation specifically protecting AI-safety whistleblowers.
