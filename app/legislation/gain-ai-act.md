The GAIN AI Act would require a company seeking a license to export advanced AI chips to China or other countries of concern to first certify that American companies and startups have had priority access to purchase those chips — only after domestic demand is satisfied could a chipmaker proceed with exports to restricted countries, subject to existing export-control law. It's aimed squarely at companies like Nvidia and AMD.

Rather than passing as a standalone bill, its most serious shot at becoming law ran through the FY2026 National Defense Authorization Act: the Senate adopted GAIN AI Act language as a floor amendment and passed its version of the NDAA 77-20, but the House's NDAA never included it, and the provision was ultimately stripped out during House-Senate conference negotiations after opposition from the White House, Nvidia, and the Semiconductor Industry Association. The standalone bills, S. 3150 and H.R. 5885, remain technically alive in committee, but with their strongest vehicle already spent, their path forward is unclear.
