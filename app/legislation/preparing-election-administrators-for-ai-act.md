The bill requires the Election Assistance Commission, in consultation with NIST, to develop voluntary guidelines to help state and local election offices address the use and risks of artificial intelligence in election administration — including cybersecurity, information sharing about elections, and the spread of AI-generated election disinformation. Compliance by election offices is voluntary; the bill creates no new obligations on AI developers or platforms and no private right of action.
