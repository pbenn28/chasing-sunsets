The AI for ALL Act (formally the Artificial Intelligence for Advancing Literacy and Learning Act) would establish a 10-member Artificial Intelligence Literacy and Education Commission within the Office of Science and Technology Policy, chaired by the OSTP Director and including representatives from OMB, NSF, Commerce, Education, Labor, and GSA, plus academic, private-sector, and AI-literacy-organization appointees. Within one year of enactment, the Commission must develop and submit to Congress a national strategy identifying opportunities and challenges for improving Americans' AI literacy, with biennial reviews and updates thereafter. It would also develop and disseminate multilingual public AI-literacy materials, including a possible public awareness campaign, and coordinate literacy efforts with state and local governments, nonprofits, and universities. The bill creates no new spending program, developer obligation, or enforcement mechanism -- it is purely an advisory-commission and strategy-development bill.
