The bill directs the Secretary of Commerce, acting through NTIA, to study how AI technology can improve the security and resilience of U.S. telecommunications networks — covering real-time threat and malware detection, network resilience, energy efficiency, integrated sensing, Open RAN security, and security risks in virtualized network environments, as well as cybersecurity risks arising from AI misuse. NTIA must consult with the FCC and industry stakeholders, take public comment, and report to House Energy and Commerce and Senate Commerce within one year of enactment, with any legislative recommendations. Reps. Menendez (D-NJ) and Pfluger (R-TX) lead a bipartisan cosponsor group; the bill cleared subcommittee by voice vote on September 1, 2026, and now awaits full committee action.
