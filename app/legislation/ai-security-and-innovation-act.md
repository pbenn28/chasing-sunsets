The bill amends the National AI Initiative Act of 2020 to establish the Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) at NIST to evaluate AI-related national and economic security risks and support U.S. AI research, development, and evaluation. (The exact expansion of “CAISI” in the amended substitute — Standards vs. Security — is reported somewhat inconsistently.)
