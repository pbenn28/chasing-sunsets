The bill directs the NTIA to conduct a study and hold public stakeholder meetings on accountability measures for AI systems — focused on communications networks, social media, and spectrum sharing — and to report findings and recommendations to Congress within 18 months, defining “accountability measures” as mechanisms such as audits, assessments, or certifications.
