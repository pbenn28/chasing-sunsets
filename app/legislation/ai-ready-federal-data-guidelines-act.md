The bill would direct NIST to develop voluntary guidelines helping federal agencies prepare open government data assets for training AI models — addressing formatting, labeling, documentation, and maintenance — with pilot programs for strategic sectors such as biotechnology. It was among ten AI bills advanced by the House Science Committee on June 25, 2026.
