The bill directs NIST to establish task forces to inform the development of technical standards and guidelines for identifying content created or manipulated by generative AI — detection, authentication, and provenance disclosure — to help consumers distinguish authentic content from AI-generated material. It was among ten AI bills advanced by the House Science Committee in June 2026.
