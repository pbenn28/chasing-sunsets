The bill would prohibit the federal government from establishing or maintaining any database that contains data collected through, or that facilitates, the biometric identity verification of a U.S. citizen, defining “biometric identity verification” to include fingerprints, iris patterns, and facial features. (The introduced text carries no short title.)
