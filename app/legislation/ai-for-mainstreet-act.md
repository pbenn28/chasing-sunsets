The bill amends Section 21(c)(3) of the Small Business Act to require SBA Small Business Development Centers (SBDCs) to provide small businesses with information, guidance, training, and outreach on AI use — covering best practices, risk planning, data protection, cybersecurity, regulatory compliance, and customer trust — building on the existing America's SBDC AI U program. It defines "artificial intelligence" by reference to the National AI Initiative Act of 2020. The introduced text authorizes no additional appropriations to carry out the mandate.

The House passed the bill 395-14 under suspension of the rules on 2026-01-20 — confirmed via the sponsor's press release and contemporaneous news coverage of the roll call. The Senate companion, S. 3586 (Young-Cantwell), has been pending in the Senate Small Business and Entrepreneurship Committee since January 2026; as of 2026-09-09, neither the House-passed bill nor its Senate companion has seen committee action in the Senate, and the bill has not been enacted.
