The bill amends the National AI Initiative Act of 2020 to support NSF-backed interdisciplinary AI fellowships and skills-based training, fostering multidisciplinary teams to develop and evaluate safe, trustworthy AI. A markup amendment expanded federal research on AI's impacts on the workforce and economy. It was among ten AI bills advanced by the House Science Committee in June 2026.
