The AWARE Act directs the FTC to develop and publicly release educational resources for parents, educators, and minors on the safe and responsible use of AI chatbots by minors — covering data-collection practices, parental supervision, and identifying safe versus unsafe use — due within 180 days of enactment.

**Update (2026-09-09):** The KIDS Act (H.R. 7757), carrying this provision, passed the full House 267-117 on 2026-06-29 and is now stalled in Senate Commerce, where Senate KOSA sponsors are pushing a competing bill with a "duty of care" standard the House package lacks — leaving this provision's ultimate fate tied to that unresolved bicameral dispute rather than to any action on H.R. 5360 itself.
