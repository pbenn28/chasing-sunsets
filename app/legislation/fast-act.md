The FAST Act would authorize the Secretary of Defense to procure software and data "as a service" (as an alternative to traditional software-as-product or data-as-supply acquisition) to support the development of AI systems. It is a narrow, permissive contracting-authority bill that expands DoD's own procurement options — it does not impose any obligation on AI developers or other private parties, and contains no preemption, reporting, or enforcement provisions.

Sponsored by Rep. Pat Fallon (R-TX) with Rep. Eugene Vindman (D-VA) as cosponsor, introduced 2025-06-06 and referred to House Armed Services, where it remains with no further action as of this writing. Given the bill's narrow scope and lack of momentum, this entry uses a lighter research pass (`text_source: summary`) rather than full statutory verification.
