The AI Training Extension Act of 2025 amends the 2022 AI Training for the Acquisition Workforce Act to expand mandatory AI training beyond acquisition personnel to a broader set of federal executive-branch employees, including supervisors, managers, and frontline staff in data and technology roles. It is a reintroduction of a similar bill Rep. Mace previously introduced with the late Rep. Gerry Connolly in the 118th Congress, which cleared the House Oversight Committee but was never enacted. As of early September 2026, this Congress's version remains at the committee-referral stage in House Oversight and Government Reform, with no markup or other action reported since its June 2025 introduction.
