The FARM AI Act (S. 4627 / H.R. 9686) directs USDA to add artificial intelligence — precision agriculture, farm management, crop production, resource management, cybersecurity, and weather monitoring — as a priority research area under the Agriculture and Food Research Initiative (AFRI) competitive grant program, and expands the Agriculture Advanced Research and Development Authority (AgARDA) to explicitly cover AI systems. It creates a new senior USDA/NIFA "AI in Agriculture Advisor" position to coordinate federal AI efforts, provide technical assistance, and work with NIST on agriculture-sector AI standards, and directs Cooperative Extension programs and workforce/technical-training grants toward AI-adoption outreach and education for farmers and rural communities. No specific appropriation figures appear in the bill text; it is a research-priority and program-authorization bill rather than an appropriations measure. Neither chamber's version has seen committee action beyond referral, and GovTrack models a near-zero enactment probability.
