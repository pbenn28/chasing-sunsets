The AI Sovereignty Act would direct the Secretary of Commerce, through the Under Secretary for Industry and Security, to report to Congress on the offshoring of critical AI research and development — including offshoring partnerships, reshoring activity, foreign acquisition of domestic AI-related assets, and the movement of U.S.-trained talent to foreign entities, including U.S. adversaries. The initial report is due within 240 days of enactment, with annual updates thereafter. It places no obligations on AI developers or other private parties; the entire obligation runs to Commerce.

The bill was introduced by Rep. Eugene Vindman (D-VA) alongside Rep. James Moylan (R-GU) as part of a three-bill national-security technology package and remains at the introductory committee-referral stage with no further action.
