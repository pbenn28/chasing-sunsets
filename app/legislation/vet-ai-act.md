The VET AI Act directs the Director of NIST to develop voluntary guidelines and specifications for internal and external assurances of AI systems, including data-privacy protections and harm mitigation. It would establish an advisory committee to recommend certification criteria for third-party AI assurance evaluators and require a NIST study of the AI assurance ecosystem, enabling independent verification of developers' safety and risk-management claims.
