The bill would direct the Secretary of Commerce, in coordination with other federal agencies, to design and carry out a nationwide public-awareness and education campaign on artificial intelligence — covering its benefits, risks, and growing prevalence in daily life. Planned content includes workforce opportunities in AI-related fields, individuals' existing legal rights regarding AI, and best practices for identifying AI-generated media such as deepfakes and chatbot-generated content. A House companion, H.R. 7151, was introduced by Reps. Nanette Barragán and Jay Obernolte in January 2026.
