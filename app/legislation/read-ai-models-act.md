The READ AI Models Act directs NIST to establish a pilot program developing voluntary resources — standardized templates and technical guidance — for documenting AI models, to improve transparency about how models are developed, evaluated, and deployed. It was among ten AI bills advanced by the House Science Committee in June 2026.
