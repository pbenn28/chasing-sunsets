The Expanding AI Voices Act would amend the National AI Initiative Act to codify and expand the National Science Foundation's ExpandAI program, directing NSF to make competitive, merit-reviewed capacity-building awards to institutions of higher education that are not among the top 100 in federal R&D expenditure, plus HBCUs, minority-serving institutions, Tribal Colleges and Universities, and eligible nonprofits (or consortia of these). Awardees may use funds for research-program development, faculty recruitment, post-baccalaureate bridge programs, computing and data-resource access, community-building activities, workshops, and integrating ethical AI practices into curricula. NSF must conduct outreach to expand the applicant pool, particularly among first-generation students and historically underrepresented groups, and must ensure awards don't duplicate existing programs. The bill creates no new agency, developer obligation, or enforcement mechanism -- it is a grant-eligibility and program-authorization bill.
